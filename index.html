Skrivist Books

Skrivist Books is a free EPUB and PDF reader that runs in your browser. Add books from your device or a folder, browse your own OPDS catalogues, or open a local Calibre library read-only. Your books, notes and reading progress stay on this device: there is no account and nothing is uploaded. Install it as an app to read offline.

Part of Skrivist. Reading comics? Try Skrivist Comics. Skrivist Cloud, coming soon, adds sync across devices and text-to-speech.
